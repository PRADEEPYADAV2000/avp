About AV Group

Building Safer Spaces Through Professional Security

AV Group Security & Facility Management is a Mumbai-based company dedicated to providing professional security and facility management services. We serve a range of clients across corporate, residential, industrial, and commercial sectors with reliable, well-trained personnel.

Our mission is to deliver dependable security solutions that allow our clients to focus on their core operations with confidence. Under the leadership of our founder, Hemant Dubey, AV Group is committed to maintaining high standards of service, discipline, and professionalism in every deployment.

Our vision is to be recognised as a trusted partner in safety and facility management across Mumbai and the broader Maharashtra region, building long-term relationships through consistent and reliable service delivery.
