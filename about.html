About AV Group

A professional security and facility management company based in Mumbai, delivering reliable protection and comprehensive facility solutions for businesses across industries.

Our Story

Building Safer Spaces Through Professional Security

AV Group Security & Facility Management is a professional security and facility management company based in Mumbai, India. Under the leadership of Hemant Dubey, AV Group provides a comprehensive range of services designed to protect businesses, residential communities, industrial facilities, and events across the city.

Our services span from trained security personnel and corporate security solutions to integrated facility management, including housekeeping and manpower supply. Each deployment is backed by thorough personnel verification, professional training, and continuous supervision to ensure consistent service quality.

AV Group is committed to understanding the unique requirements of every client and delivering customised solutions that address their specific security and operational needs. Our 24/7 operations centre ensures round-the-clock support and coordination across all active assignments in Mumbai.
